Offline Excel Progress Templates & Inputs Guide

📑 Understanding the Spreadsheet Structure

The offline Excel template workbook is designed to mirror the modules and business functions of the web interface. Grouping steps into separate tabs makes the end-to-end integration cycles exceptionally easy to understand and fill out offline.

📄 ERP_template_empty.xlsx (10 Sheets)
├── 📄 Tab 1: Progress & Config          (Student ID & Completed Step Indices)
├── 📄 Tab 2: Generated Documents         (Simulated ERP Document DB Registry)
├── 📄 Tab 3: Sales & Distribution Setup   (Setup Master Data - Step 1 to 3)
├── 📄 Tab 4: Materials Management Setup   (Component Registration - Step 4)
├── 📄 Tab 5: Sales and Distribution (Pre  (Inquiries, Quotes, Orders - Step 5 to 7)
├── 📄 Tab 6: Production Planning Setup    (Routings, Product Groups, PIRs - Step 8 to 12)
├── 📄 Tab 7: Material Requirements Plan   (MRP planning schedule - Step 13 & 14)
├── 📄 Tab 8: Procurement Process (MM)     (Purchasing component stock - Step 15 to 31)
├── 📄 Tab 9: Production Execution (PP)    (Staging, Assembly, Cost Settlement - Step 32 to 45)
└── 📄 Tab 10: Sales Fulfillment & Settle (Deliveries, billing, payment clearing - Step 46 to 54)

💡 When logging offline, enter your parameters in the columns/cells described below, then upload the workbook inside the web simulator using the Import File button at the top header to restore your state!

🔑 Tab 1: Progress & Config

Defines who you are and registers the steps you have completed. Fill in Column C (Value) for the rows:

Parameter Label (Col A) System Key (Col B) Value (Col C) Description & Real-Life Context
Student 3-Digit ID (###) `studentId` e.g., `045` Your unique training identifier. Dynamically updates all customer names, material IDs, and audit codes in the database.
Current Active Step `currentStep` e.g., `15` The sequential step number (1 to 54) you are currently working on in your ledger.
Completed Steps List `completedSteps` e.g., `1,2,3,4,5` A comma-separated list of completed step numbers. When imported, the web app marks these as completed.

🚀 Quick Guide: Importing and Synchronizing

  1. Fill out your parameters in the worksheets offline. Remember to replace all instances of ### with your assigned student ID.
  2. Open the web-based ERP Logistics Simulator.
  3. Click the Import File button in the top menu and select your Excel workbook.
  4. The system automatically parses all tabs, loads all entered values, and synchronizes your progress, so you can pick up exactly where you left off.
