1. Integrative Operations Manual

Phase 1: Customer Master Setup (Steps 1 - 3)

Prerequisite to set up the debtor financial relationship in Accounts Receivable.

Step 1: Create Business Partner (Customer)

Navigation Fiori Space: Sales Person Space → App: Manage Customer Master Data → Click Create → Select Organization. Set name to 1 Alpine Velo ###, Street to Apex Boulevard, City to Stuttgart, Postal Code to 70173, and Region to 08 (Baden-Württemberg).

Business Understanding BPs capture centralized master records. Categorizing Alpine Velo as an Organization maps legal taxation rules and enables the company to generate sales invoices and calculate freight routes in compliance with federal guidelines.

Step 2: Assign Company Code & Reconciliation Account

Navigation Navigate to the Company Code sub-view → Add Company Code ZN00 → Map Reconciliation Account G/L 11015000.

Business Understanding Reconciliation Account 11015000 acts as a general ledger bridge. Receivables transactions post directly to the sub-ledger ( Alpine Velo's account) and automatically roll up to G/L domestic receivables, maintaining continuous financial integrity.

Step 3: Define Sales & Distribution (SD) Views

Navigation Navigate to the Sales & Distribution sub-view → Add Sales Org ZN10, Channel WS, Division VL. Set wholesale prices for Commuter (1950 EUR), Endurance (3950 EUR), and Carbon Custom (4450 EUR).

Business Understanding SD views configure billing criteria and discount rules. Linking them to the proper organization ensures the system pulls accurate pricing conditions during sales transactions.

Phase 2: Materials Management Setup (Step 4)
Step 4: Create Raw Accessories Template

Navigation Fiori Space: Warehouse Supervisor → App: Manage Product Master Data → Choose Create using template ZC-REF1-###. Assign ID ZC10###, description Zenith Integrated Ride Computer, plant MU10, standard valuation price 195 EUR.

Business Understanding Establishing standard asset valuation in the Product Master guarantees that any raw component receipt immediately increments material inventory assets on the company's central balance sheet.

Phase 3: Pre-Sales & Sales Order entry (Steps 5 - 7)
Step 5: Log Customer Sales Inquiry

Navigation App: Manage Sales Inquiries → Select Inquiry Type ZI10. Input Sold-To party Alpine Velo ### and quantity parameters (10 Commuter, 5 Endurance, 3 Custom Carbon).

Business Understanding Inquiries represent non-binding negotiations. They register interest but do not allocate physical stock or create G/L liabilities, serving solely as tracking tools for commercial planning.

Step 6: Submit Binding Sales Quotation

Navigation App: Manage Sales Quotations → Click Create → Reference the Sales Inquiry generated in Step 5 to auto-fill all data fields. Add a 1-month valid-to deadline.

Business Understanding A Quotation is a legally binding contract. It commits Zenith Electro-Mobility to deliver the velos under locked wholesale pricing within the validity window, protecting the customer from price spikes.

Step 7: Post Commercial Sales Order

Navigation App: Manage Sales Orders → Click Create → Select Standard Sales Order Type ZS10. Reference the Sales Quotation to finalize the transaction.

Business Understanding The Sales Order is the core execution trigger. Saving it prompts availability checks, reserves finished stock, and establishes scheduling requirements, alerting manufacturing and warehouse clerks.

Phase 4: Production Planning & MRP Runs (Steps 8 - 14)
Step 8: Define Assembly Operations Routing

Navigation App: Manage Routings → Create Routing RO-XM10-### by copying reference template XM10-DE.

Business Understanding Routings define step-by-step manufacturing instructions (assembly work centers, setup times, machine speeds). They are critical for labor scheduling and variance calculations.

Step 13: Run MRP Live (MD01N)

Navigation App: MRP Live → Target Plant MU10 → Execute net requirement calculations.

Business Understanding Material Requirements Planning (MRP) matches supply to demand. It scans stock balances, notices the sales order shortage of raw components, and auto-generates internal purchase requisitions to drive sourcing.

Phase 5: Sourcing & Procurement purchasing (Steps 15 - 31)
Step 16: Create Supplier Master Record

Navigation App: Manage Supplier Master Data → Create supplier 1 AlpineGear Solutions ###. Assign Company Code ZN00 and Reconciliation G/L 21015000 (Trade Payables).

Business Understanding Set up the Accounts Payable relationship. G/L 21015000 tracks payables liabilities owed to vendors before invoice payment clearing.

Step 20: Post Legally Binding Purchase Order (PO)

Navigation App: Manage Purchase Orders (ME21N) → Reference the comparative selected supplier quotation to raise PO 450000###.

Business Understanding The PO is a formal procurement contract sent to AlpineGear Solutions committing to purchase raw ride computers, driving physical dispatch.

Step 21-22: Goods Receipt Post (MIGO)

Navigation App: Post Goods Receipt (MIGO) → Reference PO 450000### → Receive stock into storage location RM10 under movement MT10.

Business Understanding MIGO logs physical warehouse receipt. It debits Inventory Assets and credits GR/IR (Goods Receipt/Invoice Receipt) Clearing, recognizing inventory asset arrival before processing invoice bills.

Step 25: Supplier Invoice Matching (MIRO)

Navigation App: Create Supplier Invoice (MIRO) → Verify invoice matches PO rates and GR quantities. Post invoice INV-### for 19,800 EUR.

Business Understanding Verifies PO, GR, and Invoice matching (Three-Way Match). MIRO clears GR/IR and credits Trade Payables, establishing legal account payables debt to AlpineGear Solutions.
